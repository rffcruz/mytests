Can any rooftop become a living space?

Not every roof is suitable. Structural capacity, access, waterproofing, wind, drainage, and local requirements need professional assessment. The proposed discovery service would establish what is feasible before design or installation.

Who looks after the plants?

Canopy Works is designed around an ongoing care service, with seasonal planting, maintenance visits, and agreed responsibilities. The service scope would be tailored to each building and its facilities team.

How much would a project cost?

Pricing would follow a site assessment and agreed scope. Size, access, structural work, planting, irrigation, and maintenance all affect the budget. Start a conversation to describe your space; this concept site does not provide binding quotes.

How would we measure the benefits?

Agree a baseline and useful site measures first: planted area, plant survival, maintenance records, event participation, and harvested produce where relevant. Environmental or wellbeing outcomes would need their own evidence, rather than assumed savings.

Is Canopy Works an operating company?

This is a fictional enterprise business and website concept. Images are inspirational, the featured project is illustrative, and no customers, results, or live service commitments are represented. Chat questions are sent to the connected support service.

This is a fictional business concept. Chat is configured to send your questions to our support workflow hosted on n8n Cloud.
